Before this becomes an enclosure

The ±0.2 mm XY movement budget is an example, not a demonstrated bound. Nominal lips are inset so that their worst allowed reach stays inside the reviewed contact lands. The example side clearance consumes the full X budget, leaving no extra allowance for error or rotation. Measure actual clearance and excursion; both remain unset. Account for translation, rotation, board tolerance and print error at every contact point; H3 alone does not locate the board with zero play.

The source keeps actual thickness and final height unset. Measure both component sides and connectors, establish case stops and attachment points, and check antenna clearance. A separate jack stop or cradle plus strain relief must carry cable loads away from WIZ header joints.
