Start with the fit coupon and ZAC93 cradle. Confirm the real socket, mating header, fasteners and SMA bulkhead fit with power disconnected. Then check the other small parts before printing the large base and lid. The existing CC1101, DC adapter and fuse must be measured.

Print two pod arms. Reuse the first ZAC93 cradle if it fits. The CC1101 port blank is optional. Physical fit remains unverified.

Use millimetres, 100% scale and the supplied bed orientation. Largest part: 215 × 215 mm. The nominal TRONXY XY-3 bed is 310 × 310 mm; check the actual printable area and brim in the slicer. A generic 220 × 220 mm bed allows only 2.5 mm per edge. Assembly and exploded SCAD files are inspection models, not combined print jobs.

Factory male headers solder directly to the carrier. Keep the two service wires above the carrier in the header gap; the opening is for solder and inspection access. Physical fit remains unverified.

RECOM power assembly

Use the cleared rear band for a soft board restraint and a separate loop for cable strain relief. No mounting holes are assumed in either purchased component. Assembly details.
