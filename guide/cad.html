MC2-C classic ESP32 external-antenna carrier

MC2 is a 100 × 100 mm, two-layer, all-through-hole engineering prototype. All 19 fitted components face the room; two additional recovery footprints are unpopulated. There are no controller bodies above another component's solder tails and no carrier RF traces. Three independent factory RF connectors feed three external 2.4 GHz antennas through 50 Ω coax. This is a prototype fabrication candidate, not a production-qualified design or a claim of measured coverage.

Current power assembly override (2026-10-20): buy RECOM R-78K3.3-1.0 and its 27 µF input capacitor, not the Pololu named in the original native J2 model. J2 is a generic four-wire harness interface, not a regulator footprint: its copper, pin order and existing Gerbers remain unchanged. The replacement is installed offboard on the supported power daughterboard. Power design and qualification limits, actual harness endpoints and the full assembly guide supersede that legacy part label. The native files are retained unchanged to preserve their verified fabrication evidence; this override does not claim that the replacement hardware has been bench-qualified.

Exact controller boards

  • U1: Espressif ESP32-DevKitC-VIE, with ESP32-WROVER-IE, 8 MB flash and 8 MB quad PSRAM (4 MB mapped into the host heap, configured at 40 MHz). This is the original dual-core ESP32 generation with an external antenna connector, not an S3 and not the no-PSRAM WROOM/32UE. It has 38 pins, two rows of 19, 2.54 mm pitch and 25.40 mm row spacing. The official V4 dimension drawing gives a 27.94 × 48.26 mm carrier PCB with a projecting radio module. The official PCB layout overlays WROOM and longer WROVER outlines with the same 6.04 mm upper projection. Our body reservation includes that projection and rounding margin; the USB shell and RF center remain fit-check estimates. The official ordering table establishes the exact VIE memory/antenna variant. Do not substitute a generic 30-pin ESP32, WROVER-E with a PCB antenna, or an older 1.8 V WROVER module.
  • U2: Unexpected Maker TinyC6[D], SKU TC6D-01, 8 MB flash. This is the current Series[D] board; the discontinued fixed-U.FL TC6-U-01 is not the purchase target. Native USB adds no competing UART bridge. Solder 1×11 and 1×12 male headers to the finished module before carrier assembly. Its 23 header centers match the earlier carrier, but its PCB is 36.30 × 17.78 mm, extends farther beyond the final header row, and its RF socket is on the opposite side from the old planning estimate. The MC2-C footprint, USB shell, cable route and enclosure checks use the pinned manufacturer STEP, schematic and pinout. c6d-evidence.json records source SHA256 hashes, all header pins, coordinate transforms and the nominal RF center. The manufacturer files remain upstream. Use MC2-C RCP firmware: it selects external U.FL through the onboard FXL6408, address 0x43, XIO0=HIGH, over local GPIO6 SDA / GPIO7 SCL before enabling Thread. XIO0 is not ESP32 GPIO0; attaching the pigtail alone leaves the onboard antenna selected.
  • U3: Seeed XIAO MG24, presoldered 102010690. The module's U.FL RF path must be selected by the matching firmware: PB04 and PB05 high. A connected antenna alone does not switch the RF path. Manufacturer board source.

All three use U.FL / MHF1 mating coax, not MHF3 or MHF4. SMA bulkheads and antennas are offboard mechanical parts; their exact connector/gain/cable choices are in the RF BOM. No antenna is shared between radios.

Electrical changes and connections

pin-plan.json is the MC2-C firmware contract. All 21 application signals remain on the host: three native UARTs, shared hardware SPI, I²C, Ethernet IRQ/reset and C6 reset/boot. GPIO0 is reserved for recovery; GPIO1/3 remain on the factory USB-UART bridge; GPIO6–11 belong to flash and GPIO16/17 to PSRAM. GPIO34/35/36/39 are input-only and have no internal pull resistors. Runtime UART logging is disabled; the LD2450 UART0 is remapped to GPIO23 TX / GPIO18 RX after boot.

Host Thread TX/RX is GPIO26 / GPIO36, EN is GPIO27 and BOOT is GPIO15. TinyC6 carrier pad12 is GPIO17/ROM RX, pad13 is GPIO16/ROM TX, pad15 is EN/RESET, pad11 is GPIO9/BOOT, pad3 is 5 V, and pads2/14 are GND. R3 (10 kΩ) connects TinyC6 local3V3 (pad4) to GPIO8 (pad10): the C6 ROM downloader requires GPIO8 high together with GPIO9 low. The TinyC6 schematic leaves GPIO8 without a board pull-up; its C6-local rail must not be joined to the peripheral 3.3 V rail. Inspect the tiny board's printed labels before insertion; left and right row lengths differ. The one changed header function is carrier pad17: GPIO10 on TinyC6[D] instead of GPIO5 on the original board. It is unconnected here. GPIO6/7 also remain unconnected to the carrier, so the local antenna-control I²C bus does not join the host's sensor bus.

R4 and R5 are required boot pull-downs, not optional parts. R4 holds GPIO12 / SPI SCK LOW; the WROVER-IE datasheet, Table 2 and §4.2, specifies 3.3 V VDD_SDIO, selected by LOW on GPIO12/MTDI. R5 holds GPIO2 / SPI MOSI LOW so UART downloading works with GPIO0 LOW. Each resistor is 10 kΩ to GND. These two host outputs connect only to SPI slave inputs: never add a pull-up, LED, UART transmitter or other driving source to these nets. No eFuse modification is needed or assumed. The WIZ850io v1.1 schematic has no SCK/MOSI pull-ups; its CS, IRQ and RESET already have 4.7 kΩ pull-ups. Verify the actual CC1101 module also has no conflicting SCK/MOSI pulls before assembly.

GPIO5 carries W5500 CS and is pulled HIGH by WIZ850io. GPIO15 carries C6 BOOT; LOW at host reset only suppresses the host ROM serial log, not normal flash boot. It is normally HIGH through the C6 board's boot pull-up and host default pull-up. Do not add cross-rail pull-ups. SPI uses the GPIO matrix at the configured device speeds; the W5500 runs at 10 MHz. The MG24 four-wire UART is GPIO32 TX / GPIO39 RX / GPIO33 RTS / GPIO14 CTS. CC1101 uses GPIO13 CS / GPIO25 GDO0 open-drain TX / GPIO35 GDO2 RX; I²C is GPIO21 SDA / GPIO22 SCL.

The original ESP32 radio supports Bluetooth LE 4.2 legacy advertisements and connections. The three active BLE proxy slots remain configured, but BLE5 extended advertisements, 2M PHY and Coded PHY are not supported. Zigbee and Thread still use their independent MG24 and C6 radios. A successful build does not establish runtime heap margin or simultaneous-load reliability.

The remaining external harnesses preserve MC1's function: J1 isolated/fused 5 V input; J2 offboard 3.3 V buck (current RECOM assembly override above); J3 DFR0627 UART expander; J4 ZAC93; J5 CC1101; J6 LD2450; J7 BME680. Connectors and exact pad coordinates and net membership are generated from the same design.py as the native schematic and PCB. J8 is XIAO PC00-to-GND recovery; J9 is host GPIO0-to-GND recovery. Leave both unpopulated/open in operation.

The existing network PoE switch feeds an active, isolated, regulated 5 V splitter upstream of J1. Neither battery voltage nor raw PoE enters this board. J2 connects an external 3.3 V buck only to WIZ850io and peripheral harnesses. Controller 3.3 V outputs are not joined to that rail. Each controller receives 5 V through a separate 1N5819: cathode/banded pad1 toward the controller, anode pad2 toward the common 5 V input. Measure rail voltage at maximum simultaneous load: splitter/cable/diode losses must leave adequate local-regulator headroom. Diodes do not prevent signal-pin backpower. Use one intended power source while programming, and power down before connecting harnesses.

Mechanical contract

design.json is the geometry authority. Four 2.7 mm NPTH holes are at (4,4), (96,4), (4,96), (96,96) mm, front-view origin at the top left. Use insulating M2.5 supports no wider than 5 mm at the PCB. The WIZ850io front-facing footprint is rotated180°: its RJ45 mouth is at y102 mm, x62.45–78.55 mm, 2 mm beyond the carrier. Support its jack with the enclosure cradle so cable insertion force does not rely on header joints.

The ledger retains physical USB shells, short USB service approaches and harness-mating envelopes. The TinyC6[D] RF center is a nominal manufacturer STEP coordinate, while the host and XIAO socket points remain planning estimates; use sufficient pigtail slack and verify engagement, bend radius and strain relief with delivered boards. Never press a mismatched miniature coax connector into place. Carrier fit, connector height, printer tolerances and simultaneous-radio performance remain unmeasured. MC2 enclosure.

Checks and reproduction

Native ERC, DRC, unrouted items and schematic parity must all be zero; there are no intentional warning/overlap exemptions. The carrier has 33 connected nets. Its 38-pin host footprint is not compatible with the 44-pin MC2-A S3 footprint. MC2-C retains MC2-B’s electrical copper connections but updates the TinyC6[D] module envelope, header label and antenna firmware contract. Order the matching MC2-C board and firmware together; a zero-DRC result does not test the antenna switch or physical assembly. test_design.py also checks controller pin numbers, reserved GPIOs, supply separation and all module body/pad projections. Passive module symbols model connections, not the boards' internal power circuits.

Run with KiCad10's Python and libraries. Keep environment variables local to the task:

"$KICAD_PY" generate.py --libraries "$KICAD_LIBRARIES"
"$KICAD_CLI" sch export netlist --format kicadxml -o .build/mc2.xml mc2.kicad_sch
cp routing/mc2.ses .build/mc2.ses
"$KICAD_PY" finalize.py
"$KICAD_CLI" sch erc --severity-all --exit-code-violations --format json --output checks/erc.json mc2.kicad_sch
"$KICAD_CLI" pcb drc --severity-all --exit-code-violations --format json --schematic-parity --output checks/drc.json mc2.kicad_pcb
"$KICAD_PY" record_verification.py
python3 test_design.py
python3 render_assembly.py
python3 reproduce_check.py --python "$KICAD_PY" --cli "$KICAD_CLI" --libraries "$KICAD_LIBRARIES"

The checked-in Specctra session was routed with Freerouting1.9.0, one optimization thread. It includes every connection, including the required C6 boot pull-up and host SPI boot pull-downs. Import it with finalize.py, which also refills ground. Track/via dimensions and clearance are unchanged from MC1: 0.25 mm signal, 0.80 mm power, 0.80/0.40 mm vias, 0.20 mm clearance and 0.50 mm copper-to-edge minimum. Fabrication settings are 1.6 mm FR-4, two 35 µm copper layers, green solder mask and lead-free HASL. This is a prototype fabrication package; it is not production qualified. The native KiCad board can be used for a small prototype after the BOM, power and assembly review; physical qualification then gates a larger run.

MC2-C is the active classic ESP32 design. Physical fit, concurrent operation and coverage still require your measurements.
