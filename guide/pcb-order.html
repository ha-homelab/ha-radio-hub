Order the MC2-D prototype PCB

Order MC2-D with Seeed XIAO ESP32C6 (Pre-Soldered), 102010636, as U2. The previously selected TinyC6[D] costs $41.95 delivered in the owner's cart. The new US-stocked C6 is $6.38 before shipping and tax, with factory male headers. MC2-D replaces its footprint, recovery wiring, antenna driver and enclosure model. Do not mix MC2-B/MC2-C carrier files or TinyC6 firmware with this build.

Start with five bare PCBs and components for one complete hub. This is a prototype fabrication package. Digital checks do not establish physical fit, RF coverage, power headroom or long-term reliability; test the first assembly before purchasing electronics for a larger batch.

Final purchase check

The 21 October purchase audit reconciles the submitted DigiKey order, the Mouser host and owner-confirmed modules with MC2-D. Use the updated package whose D1 position says NO DIODE: fit a short insulated 22 AWG wire there; D2/D3 remain 1N5819. No purchased module needs replacement. The ZAC93 adapter uses soldered, individually insulated wires on the short header tails. Read these assembly corrections before fabrication.

Files to send

Upload only MC2-D-prototype-gerbers.zip to the PCB manufacturer's Gerber uploader. The accompanying manifest binds its contents to the native PCB and verification evidence.

Do not upload the previous private MC2-B-614c0cd-GERBERS-HOLD.zip, a drawing SVG, the repository ZIP or an AI-generated board image.

The archive contains front/back copper, solder mask and silkscreen, the outline, separate plated/non-plated drill files and a Gerber job file. Open both sides in the manufacturer's preview and confirm the MC2-D board marking and four mounting holes plus the rounded service window beneath U2 before submitting. This is a bare board order; it does not include mounted modules, headers or assembly.

Fabrication settings

  • One design, single boards, 100 × 100 mm, two copper layers.
  • FR-4, 1.6 mm, 1 oz copper, ordinary green solder mask and white silkscreen.
  • Lead-free HASL, tented vias, no edge plating, no castellations, no impedance control.
  • No stencil and no PCBA service. Carrier joints use through-hole soldering; two fine wires must also be soldered to XIAO underside test pads.
  • Preserve separate PTH and NPTH drill files. Do not plate the four 2.7 mm mounting holes.
  • The design uses 0.25 mm signal tracks, 0.20 mm clearance and 0.40 mm via drills; standard 6/6 mil fabrication capability is sufficient.

Use the supplied outline and drill data without scaling. Supplier DFM review may still identify manufacturing-specific requirements; resolve any question against the native KiCad source before approving a changed CAM preview.

What must match the board

  • U1: ESP32-DevKitC-VIE / WROVER-IE, 38 pins, 8 MB flash and 8 MB physical PSRAM.
  • U2: Seeed XIAO ESP32C6 (Pre-Soldered), 102010636, with two factory 1×7 male headers. DigiKey showed 21 at $6.38 on 20 October. Solder its two underside recovery leads: TP8/EN → J10.1 and TP2/BOOT9 → J10.2. The onboard GPIO8 pull-up replaces carrier R3.
  • U3: XIAO MG24, pre-soldered 102010690, with the external-antenna NCP image.
  • U4: WIZ850io; data-only Ethernet from the isolated 5 V PoE splitter.
  • Power: RECOM R-78K3.3-1.0 and its 27 µF input capacitor, on the off-board PWR1 mount. J2 remains 5 V / GND / 3.3 V / GND; see the exact connection.
  • Firmware: current MC2-D host and 4 MB XIAO ESP32C6 RCP, with GPIO3 LOW and GPIO14 HIGH for the external antenna. The updater rejects TinyC6/8 MB images; use the matched build instructions.
  • RF accessories: three Taoglas CAB.618.E pigtails and three TE ANT-2.4-LCW-SMA antennas; see the selection record.
  • Enclosure: FH1-MC2-D-PWR1 print package, with round SMA cutouts, a wall-thickness-matched coupon and shorter coax routes. The RECOM power mount remains. MC2-D revises the XIAO height, coax route and underside service-wire clearance; check the new assembly detail before fitting.

The full shopping list, carrier BOM and full-hub inventory cover the remaining parts. The received Zooz ZAC93 remains in its separate insulated cradle. Its five-wire remapped connection to J4 does not change.

After fabrication, follow Start here: verify loose parts and program the controllers, dry-fit the case, continuity-check the unpowered carrier, then test regulated power and each service before switching Home Assistant.

MC2-D is the active classic ESP32 design. Physical fit, concurrent operation and coverage still require your measurements.
