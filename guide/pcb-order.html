Order the MC2-C prototype PCB

Order MC2-C with TinyC6[D] / TC6D-01 as U2. The older MC2-B build used TC6-U-01, which is obsolete/restricted in the checked US supplier listing. The user has not bought that old board. MC2-C brings the module selection, carrier drawing, firmware antenna switch and FH1 enclosure into one revision.

Start with five bare PCBs and components for one complete hub. This is a prototype fabrication package. Digital checks do not establish physical fit, RF coverage, power headroom or long-term reliability; test the first assembly before purchasing electronics for a larger batch.

Files to send

Upload only MC2-C-prototype-gerbers.zip to the PCB manufacturer's Gerber uploader. The accompanying manifest binds its contents to the native PCB and verification evidence.

Do not upload the previous private MC2-B-614c0cd-GERBERS-HOLD.zip, a drawing SVG, the repository ZIP or an AI-generated board image.

The archive contains front/back copper, solder mask and silkscreen, the outline, separate plated/non-plated drill files and a Gerber job file. Open both sides in the manufacturer's preview and confirm the MC2-C board marking and four mounting holes before submitting. This is a bare board order; it does not include mounted modules, headers or assembly.

Fabrication settings

  • One design, single boards, 100 × 100 mm, two copper layers.
  • FR-4, 1.6 mm, 1 oz copper, ordinary green solder mask and white silkscreen.
  • Lead-free HASL, tented vias, no edge plating, no castellations, no impedance control.
  • No stencil and no PCBA service. All carrier assembly uses ordinary through-hole soldering.
  • Preserve separate PTH and NPTH drill files. Do not plate the four 2.7 mm mounting holes.
  • The design uses 0.25 mm signal tracks, 0.20 mm clearance and 0.40 mm via drills; standard 6/6 mil fabrication capability is sufficient.

Use the supplied outline and drill data without scaling. Supplier DFM review may still identify manufacturing-specific requirements; resolve any question against the native KiCad source before approving a changed CAM preview.

Quantity and delivery

The live PCBWay calculator, checked on 19 October 2026 with the settings above and United States delivery, returned:

  • 5 boards: $5.00 boards + $8.71 shipping = $13.71.
  • 10 boards: $18.18 + $12.04 = $30.22.
  • 20 boards: $51.36 + $17.71 = $69.07.

The selected service was Global Direct Shipping, quoted at 6–12 business days in addition to manufacturing. These are country-level calculator quotes, not a paid order or an address-specific total. Tax, duties and later DFM or delivery adjustments are not included. Five boards have both the lowest total and the lowest per-board cost among these checked offers.

JLCPCB showed $5.30 for five boards with lead-free HASL, but its shipping-country selector did not provide a usable delivery quote. That incomplete price is not a demonstrated cheaper delivered alternative. The observed values are stored in procurement.json.

What must match the board

  • U1: ESP32-DevKitC-VIE / WROVER-IE, 38 pins, 8 MB flash and 8 MB physical PSRAM.
  • U2: Unexpected Maker TinyC6[D] / TC6D-01, supplied male headers soldered on, no battery connector fitted. The manufacturer showed 48 available at $18 each on 19 October; check the exact SKU when purchasing.
  • U3: XIAO MG24, pre-soldered 102010690, with the external-antenna NCP image.
  • U4: WIZ850io; data-only Ethernet from the isolated 5 V PoE splitter.
  • Firmware: current MC2-C host and TinyC6[D] RCP. The latter verifies the FXL6408-controlled external antenna before starting Thread. An old TinyC6 RCP image lacks this step.
  • Enclosure: FH1-MC2-C print package, generated against the updated module body and U.FL endpoint.

The full shopping list, carrier BOM and full-hub inventory cover the remaining parts. The received Zooz ZAC93 remains in its separate insulated cradle. Its five-wire remapped connection to J4 does not change.

After fabrication, follow Start here: verify loose parts and program the controllers, dry-fit the case, continuity-check the unpowered carrier, then test regulated power and each service before switching Home Assistant.

MC2-C is the active classic ESP32 design. Physical fit, concurrent operation and coverage still require your measurements.
