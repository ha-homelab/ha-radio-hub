MC2-D pre-fabrication purchase audit — 21 October 2026

Conclusion: the confirmed purchased component variants match the corrected MC2-D carrier contract. Order five bare prototype PCBs from the current fabrication package, then qualify the first assembled hub. No purchased module needs replacement. This is a document/electrical/mechanical audit, not a claim that delivered hardware has been measured or powered.

Compatibility findings

  • U1: the ordered VIE is the 38-pin, 25.4 mm row-spacing WROVER-IE host with external antenna, 8 MB flash and PSRAM. It is not a generic 30-pin ESP32. The firmware reserves the PSRAM pins and uses the classic ESP32 target.
  • U2/U3: the exact factory-header XIAO SKUs match the 2×7, 2.54 mm pitch, 15.24 mm row-spacing footprints. Solder them directly to MC2-D; additional female sockets change the enclosure and coax geometry. C6 still needs its two fine wires from underside TP8/EN and TP2/BOOT to J10.
  • U4: WIZ850io matches the actual 12 pad assignments, its 20.32 mm row spacing and the reserved RJ45 opening. Power it from the peripheral 3.3 V rail.
  • Power: RECOM pin 1=VIN, 2=GND, 3=3.3 V; the 27 µF capacitor and PAD1 belong on the separate PWR1 assembly, not a footprint on MC2-D. J2 carries 5 V/GND/3.3 V/GND. Do not join any controller's local 3.3 V output to this peripheral rail.
  • External modules: DFR0627, ZAC93, CC1101, LD2450 and BME680 connect by the documented remapped harnesses. DFR0627 must be set to A1=1/A0=0 (0x50–0x57); BME680 remains 0x77. ZAC93 uses 3.3 V and its five-wire adapter, not a straight-through Raspberry Pi cable.
  • RF: all three CAB.618.E pigtails are U.FL/MHF-I to standard SMA female; the three ANT-2.4-LCW-SMA antennas have the matching SMA mating side. The nominal cable route and slack budget fit. Firmware must select each XIAO's external RF path.
  • Connectors: the Sullins 0.64 mm square posts and Harwin M20 contacts/housings are compatible. The 40-pin strip provides 35 fitted carrier pins. Ten crimp contacts provide six installed contacts plus four spares. The two jumper packs suffice using the explicit allocation.
  • Assembly materials: the 30 AWG wire is nominally 0.79 mm OD, within the specified 0.8 mm underside-wire limit. PAD1 must be cut to 24×24 mm with the prescribed copper-free rim. Cable ties restrain wiring; they do not replace the case's module retention hardware.

Corrections made before fabrication

D1 is an insulated 22 AWG wire link, not a 1N5819. An additional Schottky drop before the DevKitC's AMS1117 unnecessarily reduced voltage headroom. The link keeps the same hole centres, pads and copper connections. Its symbol, footprint, silkscreen, BOM and assembly instructions now identify it as a wire link. D2/D3 remain 1N5819; use two purchased diodes and keep the third as a spare. Splitter tolerance and loaded cable/contact losses still require measurement.

Flash loose boards before assembly. Do not connect powered USB to the assembled hub, including with PoE off: host USB can feed the whole 5 V bus through D1, and signals can backpower other rails. Assembled operation uses PoE and OTA.

ZAC93 adapter: insert the purchased 2×5 header's 5.84 mm long mating posts into ZAC93. Solder and individually insulate the five harness wires on its 3.05 mm short tails. Those tails are not a reliable socket-mating interface. See the five-wire pin map.

The previous CI failure was traced to stale prototype-enclosure evidence after a native design metadata change. Regeneration binds the enclosure reports to the corrected carrier; it is not evidence of a firmware compile failure.

Verification and order handoff

The final native verification report and reproduction report record ERC 0, DRC 0, unrouted 0 and schematic parity 0, with a passing clean reproduction check. The new archive passed its complete source/evidence and ten-file integrity check. The independent initial comparison of all 131 numbered PCB pads to the design contract found no pad/net discrepancies. The D1 correction preserves connected copper topology; the generated package must pass its source/evidence hash guard.

Use only MC2-D-prototype-gerbers.zip with its accompanying manifest. Follow the PCB ordering settings: five pieces, 100×100 mm, two layers, 1.6 mm FR-4, 1 oz copper, green mask, white legend, lead-free HASL, no assembly or stencil. Confirm the MC2-D marking, D1 NO DIODE legend, four NPTH mounting holes and rounded opening under U2 in the supplier preview. Old MC2-C releases and the earlier MC2-D archive with D1 shown as a diode are superseded.

Remaining physical work

Before soldering the delivered modules, dry-seat them and check header height, USB shell/tail clearance, at least 2.5 mm under C6, recovery-wire insulation and coax engagement without tension. Nominal drawings do not guarantee delivered header heights. Check the exact existing CC1101 variant and LD2450 cables.

The DigiKey order does not include the PCB, case prints, a confirmed exact 1.5 A time-delay fuse/holder, or all final fasteners/retention straps. Purchase of the selected Waveshare 27737 5 V barrel-output PoE splitter was not confirmed by this audit. The adapter and fuse-holder enclosure envelopes still need physical fitting. These are completion/fit tasks, not a discovered carrier pinout incompatibility.

After assembly, measure rails under simultaneous load, qualify thermal behavior, RF coverage, BLE/Thread/Zigbee coexistence and recovery before switching Home Assistant. The host supports legacy BLE 4.2, not BLE 5 extended advertisements. Do not treat five prototype boards as a qualification for a production batch.

MC2-D is the active classic ESP32 design. Physical fit, concurrent operation and coverage still require your measurements.
