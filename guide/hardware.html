MC2-D — three external 2.4 GHz antennas

The current engineering prototype uses an ESP32-DevKitC-VIE (WROVER-IE, 8 MB flash / 8 MB quad PSRAM) host, a XIAO ESP32C6 pre-soldered / 102010636 Thread RCP, and a XIAO MG24 Zigbee NCP. Each radio has its own external antenna. The previous MC1 Nano and MC2-A S3 remain historical and are not pin or binary compatible. All 21 application signals remain; R4/R5 constrain the classic ESP32 boot straps. Bluetooth uses legacy BLE4.2 advertising and connections, not BLE5 extended advertisements or Coded PHY.

The MC2-D selection replaces MC2-C's TinyC6[D] with the US-distributed, factory-header XIAO ESP32C6. The carrier footprint, underside recovery access window, J10 connection, RCP firmware and cable layout are revised together. See the prototype ordering record. Fit and RF acceptance must be completed on the physical assembly.

The PWR1 off-board power assembly retains RECOM R-78K3.3-1.0 plus a 27 µF input capacitor. J2 remains 5 V / GND / 3.3 V / GND. MC2-D native CAD describes this current supply; do not substitute the former Pololu as an extra purchase.

MC2-D is the active classic ESP32 design. Physical fit, concurrent operation and coverage still require your measurements.
