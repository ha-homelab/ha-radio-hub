Current MC2 documentation

Updated 20 October 2026. MC2-D is the active 100 × 100 mm carrier with separate external Zigbee, Thread and BLE antennas. The selected ESP32-DevKitC-VIE has 8 MB flash and 8 MB physical PSRAM (4 MB directly addressable); its Thread radio is XIAO ESP32C6 pre-soldered / 102010636. All components face the same side. The project is an engineering prototype, with physical and whole-house RF qualification still outstanding.

The PoE switch supplies the isolated splitter and low-voltage rails. MC2 changes neither the battery installation nor the switch supply. The FH1 enclosure adds individual mounts for power, UART bridge, Z-Wave and 433 MHz radio, a lid-mounted radar and an attached ventilated environmental-sensor pod. Its exact selected modules and unmeasured dimensions are in the assembly inventory. The selected 102010636 was in stock at DigiKey on 20 October 2026. Use the prototype ordering record and matching MC2-D firmware.

MC1 remains a historical build. Its software checks continue for regression coverage, but its Nano/H2 pin map, 16 MB flash layout, compact housing and cost estimate must not be combined with MC2.

MC2-D is the active classic ESP32 design. Physical fit, concurrent operation and coverage still require your measurements.
