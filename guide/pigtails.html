MC2-C pigtail selection

Checked 19 October 2026 for one prototype delivered to the United States. Buy 3 × Taoglas CAB.618.E, one each for BLE, Thread and Zigbee. Keep the three TE/Linx ANT-2.4-LCW-SMA antennas. This replaces the previously selected Amphenol cables without changing the carrier PCB or radio firmware. Use the matching FH1-MC2-C-RF1 enclosure and connector coupon.

Exact part and observed cost

DigiKey 931-CAB.618.E-ND showed 48 in stock and $4.66 each, so three cost $13.98 before shipping, tax and any tariff. Against the previous $29.47-per-cable offer, the reduction is $74.43 for one hub ($88.41 → $13.98, about 84%). This is an observed offer, not reserved stock or a checkout total; DigiKey specifically states that a US tariff may appear in the cart.

The Taoglas product page identifies a 150 mm, 1.13 mm micro-coax assembly with a right-angle I-PEX MHF I / U.FL-compatible radio connection and a straight standard-polarity SMA female bulkhead. The SMA end has a center socket; the selected antenna has the mating center pin. Do not substitute RP-SMA or the smaller MHF4/IPEX4 connector.

The alternatives may work, but they are not the released enclosure's selected assembly. Savings alone do not establish that a different bulkhead, nut or cable will fit. The existing unverified AliExpress offers remain sample candidates. In particular, Adafruit's linked C934-001 drawing labels both “SMA Female RP” and “RP - SMA Female”. That conflicts with the standard-SMA product description; it is not proof that the item actually shipped has reverse polarity.

RF evidence and its limits

Taoglas publishes a 1.13 mm black micro-coax specification with 50 ±3 Ω, 6.8 mm minimum bend radius, and attenuation of 3.1 dB/m at 2400 MHz. RF1 uses these cable-type values as its engineering basis; the checked sources do not prove that this exact wire part is used in every CAB.618.E manufacturing lot. Multiplying by the nominal 0.150 m gives an estimated 0.465 dB cable-only loss. This excludes both connectors, mismatch and assembly variation; it is not a measured insertion-loss result for the complete CAB.618.E or this hub.

Manufacturer geometry references are the cable assembly catalog, CAB.618.E drawing on PDF page 11 and a published CAB.618.E STEP link. The STEP download was blocked and was not inspected; no dimensions were taken from it. The catalog groups the 150 mm CAB.618.E with several cable lengths, but its shared drawing labels 200 ±6 mm. It does not establish an exact 150 mm length tolerance. The project therefore reserves a 6 mm shortfall allowance, giving a 144 mm minimum design budget. This is a conservative engineering assumption, not a supplier guarantee; check the received cable.

The same family drawing specifies a 14.8 ±0.3 mm SMA body, 11.4 ±0.3 mm threaded portion, 1.75 ±0.2 mm nut and 1.1 ±0.3 mm washer, with a 1/4-36 UNS-2A thread. These dimensions support the enclosure review, while the final nut position and antenna engagement must be checked physically. RF1 provides 6.8 mm round bulkhead holes and a coupon with the same 3 mm mounting thickness as the enclosure. Its routing reserves 25 mm service slack within the 144 mm design budget. Use the current full-hub assembly instructions, connector coupon and generated fit report for the corresponding mounting and routing. Do not mix an older coupon or routing illustration with the new cable.

Before closing the first enclosure, check the delivered cable against its specified dimensions, mount the bulkhead without twisting the micro-coax, and confirm that the cable reaches with service slack and bends no tighter than 6.8 mm radius. Seat each miniature connector squarely; leave no tension on the radio socket. Confirm usable antenna engagement with the supplied washer and nut. The drawing review does not replace this first-article fit check or an in-home BLE/Thread/Zigbee coverage test.
