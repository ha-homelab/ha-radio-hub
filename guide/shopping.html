MC2-D: parts, availability and observed prices

Updated 20 October 2026 for one MC2-D hub with the PWR1 power assembly. MC2-D replaces the Australian TinyC6[D] order with the Seeed XIAO ESP32C6 pre-soldered SKU 102010636. Buy factory-fitted headers/connectors wherever available. The carrier BOM, RF BOM and assembly guide describe the same revision.

The two ESP boards and the Zigbee board

  1. 1 × Espressif ESP32-DevKitC-VIE, complete development board with ESP32-WROVER-IE, male headers, 8 MB flash and 8 MB physical PSRAM. The original ESP32 maps 4 MB of that PSRAM for capability-based allocation; this project uses quad PSRAM at 40 MHz. The IE module has the factory external antenna connector. Buy the 38-pin board (two 19-pin rows), not a loose module, WROOM board, onboard-antenna VE or an S3 board. It runs Ethernet, the active BLE 4.2 proxy and Thread Border Router. Espressif's exact ordering table confirms the memory and antenna variant. Mouser exact-part listing showed $9.48 for one and 488 in stock on its live US page on 19 October 2026. Confirm the offer and delivery at checkout. This host is already ordered. No exact AliExpress VIE variant has been qualified for MC2-D. The previous S3 offer was $15.20 promotional / $22.00 crossed-out price, before $5.93 shipping: the observed board-only reduction is $5.72 (37.6%) against that promotional price, not a delivered-cost quote. This purchase requires the MC2-D carrier and initial USB-UART flash; it is not a drop-in replacement on the older MC2-A PCB.

  2. 1 × Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32C6 (Pre-Soldered), SKU 102010636. DigiKey 1597-102010636-ND showed $6.38 and 21 in stock on 20 October 2026. Select 102010636, with two factory-soldered 7-pin male headers. Do not substitute the unsoldered 113991254, three-pack 102010574, or TinyC6. No extra female sockets are used in the current direct-solder carrier height.

    This 4 MB board is the dedicated MC2-D Thread RCP. It has native USB and a switched external U.FL path. MC2-D firmware sets GPIO3 LOW, GPIO14 HIGH before Thread starts; TinyC6's I²C-expander firmware is incompatible. Manufacturer pin map and RF switch.

    Two small solder joints remain on the module: EN and BOOT are underside pads, not header pins. Fit flexible insulated 30 AWG leads from TP8/EN to J10.1 and TP2/BOOT to J10.2, with strain relief and the carrier's service window. These are required for the existing automatic reset and network RCP update/recovery functions. Do not omit them or confuse the bottom view with a top view. Follow the assembly guide. GPIO8 already has a local pull-up on XIAO; the former carrier R3 is removed. No battery connector or battery is needed.

  3. 1 × Seeed Studio XIAO MG24, pre-soldered, SKU 102010690. This is a Silicon Labs EFR32MG24 board, not an ESP32. It runs our external-antenna Ember Zigbee firmware. The Sense variant is not needed. DigiKey exact part showed $8.90 and 16 in stock on 20 October 2026. AliExpress candidate is a mixed XIAO-family listing found in search; its live page failed to load, so its MG24/pre-soldered variant, seller, price and stock are unverified. Prefer the exact DigiKey SKU until those details are checked.

Power parts: retain the splitter, replace the expensive buck

Buy 1 × RECOM R-78K3.3-1.0 and 1 × Panasonic EEU-FS1H270 input capacitor. The RECOM costs $3.98 instead of the previous Pololu's $26.95; the capacitor adds $0.40, so the replacement power parts total $4.38. The observed reduction is $22.57 including the new capacitor, before mounting materials, shipping and tax. Do not buy the old D24V22F3 as well.

The existing Waveshare POE-SPLITTER-25W-5.5-2.1, SKU 27737 remains the selected isolated 5 V DC-barrel splitter. Its fused 5 V output supplies the controller boards and LD2450 directly, and also feeds the RECOM through J2. The RECOM supplies 3.3 V peripherals: WIZ850io, DFR0627, ZAC93, CC1101 and BME680. The development boards use their own regulators; their local 3.3 V outputs must remain separate.

  • RECOM R-78K3.3-1.0, DigiKey 945-R-78K3.3-1.0-ND: 4.5–36 V input, 3.3 V / 1 A output, SIP-3, 11.5 × 7.55 × 10.2 mm body. Its exact input range includes the splitter's 5 V output. Do not substitute R-78E3.3-0.5, which needs at least 6 V. See the RECOM datasheet.
  • Panasonic EEU-FS1H270, DigiKey P122402-ND: 27 µF, 50 V, ±20%, radial electrolytic, 105 °C, FS-A series. The 50 V marking is the capacitor's rating; this assembly still receives 5 V only. Body is nominally Ø5 × 11 mm, with Ø5.5 × 12.5 mm reserved for tolerance, and 2 mm lead spacing. Its 5,000-hour rating at 105 °C is an endurance specification, not a promise about the lifetime of the complete hub. Panasonic datasheet.
  • 1 × 24 × 24 × 1.6 mm isolated-pad perfboard cutoff, 2.54 mm pitch, plus insulated hookup wire, individual heat-shrink sleeves and the printed PWR1 tray/restraints. Suitable workshop stock can be reused; these materials are not priced below. Use the actual PWR1 pad/wire layout, not uncut stripboard.

The capacitor is fitted close to VIN and GND for the datasheet's hard-plugging condition: positive to VIN, negative to GND. J2 remains the four-wire carrier interface: J2.1 → VIN, J2.2/J2.4 → GND, J2.3 ← VOUT. The RECOM itself has only three pins; pin 1 = VIN, 2 = GND, 3 = VOUT. Follow the assembly drawing for orientation, lead insulation, polarity checks and the initial load test. This is an engineering assembly selection; physical startup and thermal tests remain due.

Three antennas and three pigtails

The current engineering selection, with the FH1-MC2-D-PWR1 enclosure, is:

  • 3 × TE Connectivity / Linx ANT-2.4-LCW-SMA (manufacturer, DigiKey listing). 2400–2485 MHz, 50 Ω, 2.8 dBi peak gain, 83.1 mm long, standard SMA male with a center pin. One each for BLE, Thread and Zigbee. DigiKey showed $3.76 each, 2,777 available; three total $11.28.
  • 3 × Taoglas CAB.618.E (manufacturer, DigiKey 931-CAB.618.E-ND). 150 mm, 50 Ω, 1.13 mm coax, I-PEX MHF I/U.FL-compatible right-angle board plug to straight standard SMA female bulkhead with a center socket. DigiKey showed $4.66 each, 48 available; three total $13.98. This saves $74.43 against the previous three Amphenol cables at $88.41. Use the RF1 round-hole coupon and revised cable routing. The selection record explains the length allowance, bend radius and physical checks; the supplier's shared drawing does not guarantee the exact 150 mm SKU's length tolerance.

AliExpress options for a lower-cost sample, not qualified BOM replacements:

  • BEVOTOP pigtail listing was inspected live. Select exactly Color: SMA F to RF1 F and Pins: 15CM, quantity 3. The selected variant displayed $3.84; the listing displayed $1.68 shipping before checkout. This selection describes standard SMA and first-generation miniature coax, not RP-SMA or RF4/MHF4. Seller naming of miniature plug gender is inconsistent: verify the mating geometry against U.FL, not the word “female” alone. Its loss, bulkhead dimensions and physical mating fit have not been measured; repeat the connector coupon/dry-fit checks before adopting it.
  • ALLiSHOP 2.4 GHz, 3 dBi SMA male (pin) antenna candidate. A matching title was found in search, but the live page stopped at CAPTCHA. Its currently selected variant, dimensions, stock, price and RF performance are not verified. Do not treat this as the TE antenna or order from the title alone. The exact TE antenna above remains the preferred selection.

The target chain is radio U.FL → dedicated 150 mm pigtail → SMA female bulkhead → SMA male 2.4 GHz antenna. Repeat it three times. There is no combiner. Do not buy RP-SMA, IPEX4/MHF4, 433 MHz or 868/915 MHz antennas for these three paths. The existing CC1101 still needs its separate matching 433.92 MHz antenna; ZAC93 keeps its original regional antenna and LD2450 its printed radar array.

Before committing the full parts order

Order the MC2-D fabrication package, with 102010636 as U2. MC2-C used TinyC6[D]; its Gerbers and RCP image are not substitutes for MC2-D. The revision and ordering record ties the current PCB, firmware and enclosure together. Start with five bare PCBs and components for one complete prototype; the extra PCBs are spares, not a reason to buy five untested sets of electronics.

Also retain or source 1 × WIZ850io, the isolated PoE splitter, regulated 5 V output plus the RECOM 3.3 V assembly, fuses, DFR0627, regional ZAC93, CC1101, LD2450, BME680 and harnesses described in the assembly guide. Use the priced lines and explicit exclusions above together. The FH1 full-hub inventory and assembly guide select the finished Waveshare POE-SPLITTER-25W-5.5-2.1 and specify the modules, power accessories and mounts for the complete enclosure. The ZAC93 has been received; do not order a second one for this build. Existing CC1101 and other delivered-part dimensions still need to be checked against the reserved envelopes.

Connect antennas before powering the radios; the README's assembled concept omits cables to show the board. Actual pinout and routing come from CAD, not the generated images.

MC2-D is the active classic ESP32 design. Physical fit, concurrent operation and coverage still require your measurements.
