MC2-B: exact radio and antenna shopping list

Checked 18 October 2026 for one MC2 hub. This is the radio/antenna purchase list, not the complete hub BOM. The carrier BOM, RF BOM and assembly guide remain the engineering references. No order has been placed. Prices below are observed offers, not checkout totals; discount eligibility, tax, delivery and stock can change.

The two ESP boards and the Zigbee board

  1. 1 × Espressif ESP32-DevKitC-VIE, complete development board with ESP32-WROVER-IE, male headers, 8 MB flash and 8 MB physical PSRAM. The original ESP32 maps 4 MB of that PSRAM for capability-based allocation; this project uses quad PSRAM at 40 MHz. The IE module has the factory external antenna connector. Buy the 38-pin board (two 19-pin rows), not a loose module, WROOM board, onboard-antenna VE or an S3 board. It runs Ethernet, the active BLE 4.2 proxy and Thread Border Router. Espressif's exact ordering table confirms the memory and antenna variant. Mouser exact-part listing was indexed at $9.48 for one on 18 October 2026. Its live page blocked automated rechecking, so confirm the current offer, stock and delivery at checkout. No exact AliExpress VIE variant has been qualified for MC2-B. The previous S3 offer was $15.20 promotional / $22.00 crossed-out price, before $5.93 shipping: the observed board-only reduction is $5.72 (37.6%) against that promotional price, not a delivered-cost quote. This purchase requires the MC2-B carrier and initial USB-UART flash; it is not a drop-in replacement on the older MC2-A PCB.

  2. 1 × Unexpected Maker TinyC6 with fixed U.FL, order code TC6-U-01. Project documents call this TinyC6-U-01. It is the original board with 8 MB flash, native USB and supplied loose 1×12 + 1×11 headers. It runs the dedicated Thread RCP. AliExpress supply was not found and immediate availability remains unconfirmed. Mouser exact-part listing was previously on backorder; its live stock page could not be rechecked. The fresh Kamami listing now redirects into retired products. Old search snippets showing stock are not a purchase confirmation. Secure an actual TC6-U-01 before ordering carriers. Do not substitute TC6-01, TinyC6[D]/TC6D-01, XIAO ESP32C6, ESP32-C6 SuperMini or an H2 board: the present carrier and firmware are tied to this layout. Original manufacturer design files document the original board; the newer Series D antenna selection uses a different software-controlled RF path.

  3. 1 × Seeed Studio XIAO MG24, pre-soldered, SKU 102010690. This is a Silicon Labs EFR32MG24 board, not an ESP32. It runs our external-antenna Ember Zigbee firmware. The Sense variant is not needed. Seeed exact part shows $8.90 and in-stock status in the checked product page. AliExpress candidate is a mixed XIAO-family listing found in search; its live page failed to load, so its MG24/pre-soldered variant, seller, price and stock are unverified. Prefer the exact Seeed part until those details are checked.

Three antennas and three pigtails

The exact existing MC2 engineering selection is:

  • 3 × TE Connectivity / Linx ANT-2.4-LCW-SMA (manufacturer, DigiKey listing). 2400–2485 MHz, 50 Ω, 2.8 dBi peak gain, 83.1 mm long, standard SMA male with a center pin. One each for BLE, Thread and Zigbee.
  • 3 × Amphenol RF 095-902-545-150 (manufacturer, Mouser listing). 150 mm, 50 Ω, 1.13 mm coax, AMC/U.FL-compatible right-angle board plug to straight standard SMA female bulkhead with a center socket. These are the specific bulkhead dimensions used by the enclosure fit coupon.

AliExpress options for a lower-cost sample, not qualified BOM replacements:

  • BEVOTOP pigtail listing was inspected live. Select exactly Color: SMA F to RF1 F and Pins: 15CM, quantity 3. The selected variant displayed $3.84; the listing displayed $1.68 shipping before checkout. This selection describes standard SMA and first-generation miniature coax, not RP-SMA or RF4/MHF4. Seller naming of miniature plug gender is inconsistent: verify the mating geometry against U.FL, not the word “female” alone. Its loss, bulkhead dimensions and physical mating fit have not been measured; repeat the connector coupon/dry-fit checks before adopting it.
  • ALLiSHOP 2.4 GHz, 3 dBi SMA male (pin) antenna candidate. A matching title was found in search, but the live page stopped at CAPTCHA. Its currently selected variant, dimensions, stock, price and RF performance are not verified. Do not treat this as the TE antenna or order from the title alone. The exact TE antenna above remains the preferred selection.

The target chain is radio U.FL → dedicated 150 mm pigtail → SMA female bulkhead → SMA male 2.4 GHz antenna. Repeat it three times. There is no combiner. Do not buy RP-SMA, IPEX4/MHF4, 433 MHz or 868/915 MHz antennas for these three paths. The existing CC1101 still needs its separate matching 433.92 MHz antenna; ZAC93 keeps its original regional antenna and LD2450 its printed radar array.

Before committing the full parts order

The unresolved item is the original TC6-U-01. The board design is digitally checked, but that does not establish supply. If the original cannot be sourced, the next engineering step is to qualify an available C6 board and update the carrier, recovery wiring and firmware together. Ordering a lookalike first does not resolve this dependency.

Also retain or source 1 × WIZ850io, the isolated PoE splitter, regulated 5 V/3.3 V supplies, fuses, DFR0627, regional ZAC93, CC1101, LD2450, BME680 and harnesses described in the assembly guide. They are not included in the radio prices above. The FH1 full-hub inventory and assembly guide select the finished Waveshare POE-SPLITTER-25W-5.5-2.1 and specify the modules, power accessories and mounts for the complete enclosure. The ZAC93 has been received; do not order a second one for this build. Existing CC1101 and other delivered-part dimensions still need to be checked against the reserved envelopes.

Connect antennas before powering the radios; the README's assembled concept omits cables to show the board. Actual pinout and routing come from CAD, not the generated images.

MC2-B is the active classic ESP32 design. Physical fit, concurrent operation and coverage still require your measurements.
