# Sources and verification limits

<!-- MC2-CURRENT-DESIGN -->
> **Current build: [MC2 — three external antennas](../docs/external-antennas.md).** This page retains an earlier design or evaluation for reference. Its board images, pin maps, enclosure fit and alternative BOMs are not the current MC2 build.

Checked on 2026-10-16. The sources below support individual components and protocols; they do not verify combined operation, electromagnetic compatibility or certification of this enclosure.

- [Waveshare PoE splitter 25W](https://www.waveshare.com/product/accessories/power-heat-sinks/poe-splitter-25w-type-c.htm) — DC-output model, nominal 5 V with 5 A maximum, isolation and dimensions. This does not establish a guarantee of continuous 25 W in our enclosure.
- [DC-version splitter photo](https://www.waveshare.com/img/devkit/accBoard/POE-SPLITTER-25W-5.5-2.1/POE-SPLITTER-25W-5.5-2.1-details-pack.jpg) — captive male RJ45 and male DC leads: no additional Ethernet patch cable is needed between splitter and switch.
- [Omada POE160S / TL-POE160S](https://www.omadanetworks.com/us/business-networking/omada-accessory-poe-adapter/poe160s/) — selected external IEEE 802.3af/at source up to 30 W when no PoE+ port is available; built-in PSU and included mains cable.
- [TP-Link TL-SF1005D v13](https://www.tp-link.com/us/home-networking/5-port-switch/tl-sf1005d/v13/) — 100 Mbps, 104 × 70 × 22 mm, 5 V supply and maximum consumption of 1.9 W; the budget uses 2 W pending measurement.
- [Raspberry Pi 4](https://www.raspberrypi.com/products/raspberry-pi-4-model-b/) — selected Linux platform. Consumption in the project calculation is an assumption, not a quoted datasheet maximum.
- [Exact SLZB-07Mg24 model](https://smlight.tech/products/slzb-07mg24) — 18 × 54 × 10 mm; [SMLIGHT firmware settings](https://smlight.tech/support/manuals/books/slzb-07pxmgx/page/zigbee2mqtt-setup) — hardware RTS/CTS; [SMLIGHT benchmark](https://smlight.tech/blog/zigbee-over-ethernet-vs-usb-latency-test) — 115200 baud for the tested NCP.
- [SMLIGHT series overview](https://smlight.tech/support/manuals/books/slzb-06xmrxmrxuultima-series/page/slzb-series-overview) and [SLZB-07 family](https://smlight.tech/products/slzb-07) — USB family, Mg24 variant. Some older promotional pages mix SLZB-06 PoE and SLZB-07 specifications; verify the exact SKU before buying rather than ordering from a search-result title alone.
- [Nordic nRF52840 Dongle](https://www.nordicsemi.com/Products/Development-hardware/nRF52840-Dongle) — PCA10059; [OpenThread platform build](https://github.com/openthread/openthread/blob/main/src/posix/README.md) and [OTBR preparation](https://openthread.io/guides/border-router/prepare) — USB RCP and required USB bootloader layout.
- [Waveshare ESP32-S3-ETH](https://www.waveshare.com/product/iot-communication/wired-comm-converter/esp32-s3-eth.htm), [wiki](https://www.waveshare.com/wiki/ESP32-S3-ETH), [schematic](https://files.waveshare.com/wiki/ESP32-S3-ETH/ESP32-S3-ETH-Schematic.pdf) — 16 MB/8 MB, W5500, GPIO/antenna selector. The schematic and wiki do not always label SD MOSI/MISO consistently; this design does not install an SD card.
- [Current Waveshare documentation](https://docs.waveshare.com/ESP32-S3-ETH) — SKU 34882 with soldered headers; [dimension drawing](https://docs.waveshare.com/assets/images/ESP32-S3-ETH-details-size-e17a2432fc60e98e2ed27afcdbea08cc.webp) — 72.8 × 21 mm in plan.
- [Zooz ZAC93 manual](https://www.getzooz.com/downloads/zooz-z-wave-800-series-long-range-gpio-module-zac93-LR-manual.pdf), [hardware changes](https://www.support.getzooz.com/kb/article/1389-zac93-gpio-module-change-log/) and [ZAC93 ESP32 project](https://github.com/ha-homelab/zac93-esp32) — 3.3 V, UART and reset-pin revisions. Manual: 0…40 °C; page 2, §2.7/2.9 set transmitter co-location/evaluation conditions; see the [prototype limitation](antennas.md). Public project documentation at commit `a6137271b947bb3f52d472d64b15be25a4220039` was reviewed locally.
- [RF Airbridge](https://github.com/ha-homelab/rf-airbridge) — ESP8266/CC1101, SPI/GDO0, MQTT/API and receiver-patch version. The initial hardware snapshot is `e70d5ef310baed710239ca1be97dee5eead3697f`; the completed RF software port uses `c96777d0df65a7420e4883d79187f31eb1b98c9b`; see [migration](rf-migration.md). [TI CC1101](https://www.ti.com/product/CC1101) provides chip specifications, which do not replace the specifications of a complete RF module.
- [ESPHome LD2450](https://esphome.io/components/sensor/ld2450/) — 24 GHz tracking, UART 256000 and required firmware. Official Hi-Link files are linked from this page; measure the dimensions of the actual delivered unit before printing.
- [Adafruit BME680 3660](https://www.adafruit.com/product/3660) and [guide](https://learn.adafruit.com/adafruit-bme680-humidity-temperature-barometic-pressure-voc-gas?view=all) — breakout power, I²C address and the distinction between raw gas resistance and calculated air-quality indicators.
- [Adafruit 4397](https://www.adafruit.com/product/4397) — separate 150 mm QT → female-socket cable; [official QT EagleCAD](https://github.com/adafruit/Adafruit-BME680-PCB/blob/master/Adafruit%20BME680.brd) — 25.4 × 17.78 mm PCB outline, excluding projecting plugs.
- [ESPHome BLE proxy](https://esphome.io/components/bluetooth_proxy/) — Ethernet as the preferred BLE uplink, radio/connection limits.
- [HA ZHA](https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/zha/), [HA OTBR](https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/otbr/), [HA Thread](https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/thread/), [HA Matter](https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/matter/) — integration roles and commissioning.

- [DFRobot FIT0993](https://www.dfrobot.com/product-2831.html) — terminal input, USB-C output, 5 V / 2 A, 300 mm. Selected for both S3 boards.
- [Adafruit 4536](https://www.adafruit.com/product/4536) — DC → USB-C; the manufacturer lists Pi 4, but no separate 3 A rating, PD or protection. [Same Sky PP3-002A datasheet](https://www.sameskydevices.com/product/resource/pp3-002a.pdf) — 5.5/2.1 mm power plug, 6 A; that rating does not extend to 4536. Harness assembly and load testing are described in [procurement](procurement.md).

Pricing: the supplier pages showed $19.99 for the splitter, $16.99–25.99 for the S3 family, $18.95 for BME680, $0.95 for the QT cable, $5.50 for Adafruit 4536 and $3.90 for each FIT0993. Other CSV ranges are **engineering budget allowances**, not discovered offers; blank prices were not quoted. Tax/shipping, tools, spare parts and Thread end devices are excluded. Recheck SKU and availability before buying. The status of selected power parts, pending connector checks and fuses is recorded in [procurement](procurement.md).
