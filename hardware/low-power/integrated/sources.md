# LP-I0 source and footprint record

> **Current build: [MC2 — three external antennas](../../../docs/external-antennas.md).** This page retains an earlier design or evaluation for reference. Its board images, pin maps, enclosure fit and alternative BOMs are not the current MC2 build.

Checked on 2026-10-17. Dimensions are millimetres, viewed from the **PCB component side** unless explicitly stated otherwise. Public source drawings remain the property of their publishers; they are linked here rather than redistributed. The current release gate requires independent review against the latest manufacturer revision.

- **ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N16R8:** [Espressif datasheet](https://www.espressif.com/sites/default/files/documentation/esp32-s3-wroom-1_wroom-1u_datasheet_en.pdf), module pad numbers. The `RF_Module:ESP32-S3-WROOM-1` land pattern is from the KiCad 10.0.6 distribution. Native USB is on GPIO19/20; module pads13/14. Flash/PSRAM pins are not general-purpose connections.
- **ESP32-H2-MINI-1-H4S:** [Espressif datasheet](https://www.espressif.com/sites/default/files/documentation/esp32-h2-mini-1_mini-1u_datasheet_en.pdf), v1.6. The vendored land pattern is from [Espressif's KiCad repository](https://github.com/espressif/kicad-libraries/blob/dd76561812ab300351234ba6e0ec1295641796f0/footprints/Espressif.pretty/ESP32-H2-MINI-1.kicad_mod), pinned commit `dd76561812ab300351234ba6e0ec1295641796f0`. Its native geometry and ground pads are retained. Standard module VBAT is internally connected to 3V3; its external pad is not tied a second time.
- **MGM240PA22VNA3:** [Silicon Labs datasheet](https://www.silabs.com/documents/public/data-sheets/mgm240p-datasheet.pdf). The accessible, primary-authored [distributor mirror](https://download.kamami.pl/p1186191-MGM240P-Datasheet.pdf) is **Rev1.0**, not a verified current revision. Figure8.3 defines the land pattern used here: body12.9×15.0, side pad centers x0.8/12.1, 1.0mm pitch, bottom center row y14.2. Side pads2.4×0.6; bottom0.6×2.4; antenna keepout x2.05..10.85/y0..4.8. Figure8.2 is a bottom view and must not be copied without mirroring. Current-revision confirmation remains a release gate.
- **W5500:** [WIZnet v1.1.0 datasheet](https://docs.wiznet.io/img/products/w5500/W5500_ds_v110e.pdf), [magjack reference schematic](https://docs.wiznet.io/img/products/w5500/w5500_sch_v110_use_mag_.pdf), and [hardware design guide](https://docs.wiznet.io/Design-Guide/hardware_design_guide). Use `LQFP-48_7x7mm_P0.5mm`; do not substitute a QFN land pattern. Datasheet pin23 (RSVD) is tied to GND. The reference defines the receiver AC coupling, center-tap bias, 25MHz resonator circuit and internal-regulator capacitors. The design guide requires short, matched MDI pairs and no layer changes; any routed exceptions must be resolved before fabrication release.
- **Bel0813-1X1T-57-F:** [manufacturer drawing](https://www.belfuse.com/media/drawings/products/magjack%20ICMs/dr-mag-0813-1x1t-57-f.pdf), RevF, sheet4 explicitly **component-side view**. Four cable-side PoE taps are separate from the common PHY-side center tap. Pads3/4/12 are physically absent. Locator holes are 12.70mm apart; signal rows y15.24/17.78 from locator centers. A 0.90mm signal drill and1.50mm land are used, within the drawing's drill tolerance and with0.30mm radial annulus. Shield and locator dimensions must remain independent of electrical contact numbers.
- **Ag97005-FL:** [Silvertel Ag97000 datasheet](https://silvertel.com/images/datasheets/Ag97000-Datasheet-low-cost-isolated-12W-Power-over-Ethernet-PoE-module-solution.pdf), v2.0 September2026, Figures2/14 and Table3. Pin1..6 pitch2.54mm; pin6-to-pin7 gap30.48mm; pin7..10 pitch2.54mm. Drill1.12mm. The drawing specifies a5.5mm-wide copper exclusion corridor, extending more than7.46mm above and3mm below the pin row; this board reserves6×22mm. Product power is9W for the5V variant. The module's isolation rating does not qualify the surrounding PCB.
- **Pololu D24V22F3:** [product](https://www.pololu.com/product/2857), [dimension drawing](https://www.pololu.com/file/0J1031/d24v22fx-step-down-voltage-regulator-dimension-diagram.pdf), [manufacturer photo](https://a.pololu-files.com/picture/0J6802.1200.jpg). The drawing mixes a top view with bottom-side labels: the pattern here uses the top view, main row at y16.53. PG/EN/VIN/GND/VOUT centers startx1.27 at2.54pitch. ExtraGND=(1.27,5.84). Module board17.8×17.8; mounting holes=(2.3,2.3)/(15.5,15.5), drill2.18. Header drills1.02. These are symbolic names, not manufacturer-assigned numerical pins.
- **SC16IS752IPW,128:** [NXP datasheet](https://www.nxp.com/docs/en/data-sheet/SC16IS752_SC16IS762.pdf), TSSOP-28 pin table. **TPS3808G33DBVR:** [TI datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps3808.pdf), SOT23-6. **ABLS-3.6864MHZ-B2-T:** [Abracon](https://abracon.com/parametric/crystals/ABLS-3.6864MHZ-B2-T). Load capacitors22pF/33pF are starting values, not a measured oscillator qualification.
- **USBLC6-2SC6:** [ST datasheet DS4260 Rev7](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/usblc6-2.pdf), SOT23-6. I/O1=1/6, GND=2, I/O2=3/4, VBUS=5. Its protection rating is at component level. USB receptacle `TYPE-C-31-M-12` uses the KiCad HRO footprint, including four `SH` pads; verify the purchased connector drawing before assembly.
- **Keyed headers:** [JST XH](https://www.jst-mfg.com/product/pdf/eng/eXH.pdf) and [JST SH](https://www.jst-mfg.com/product/pdf/eng/eSH.pdf). XH2.50mm and SH1.00mm are different families. The cable mating view is mirrored relative to the PCB top view.
