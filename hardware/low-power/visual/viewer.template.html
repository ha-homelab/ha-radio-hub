Power path

Active PoE PSE → isolated PoE front end → protected 5 V rail → 3.3 V buck.

5 V: LD2450 and buck input.

3.3 V: S3, W5500, H2, MG24, SC16, ZAC93, CC1101 and BME680.

No PoE input voltage may reach a GPIO. WIZ850io headers do not expose PoE center taps.

Communication paths

Before applying power

Verify connector orientation, continuity, rail polarity and the exact board revision. Separate external 3.3 V from development-board USB/5 V regulator outputs.

Check USB–UART conflicts on S3 GPIO43/44 and H2 GPIO23. Check the S3 GPIO48 LED conflict.

Use the PCB revision's own connector map for carrier sockets. This atlas labels module endpoints.
