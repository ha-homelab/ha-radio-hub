The selected architecture

The former C0/M0, I0 and D0 paths are historical references. The all-H2 experiment was not selected because its local border-router implementation and Zigbee integration/backup behavior did not meet the compatibility priority.

Direct solder mounting

  1. Match exact module revisions to the drawings. Program each module before permanent assembly.
  2. Dry-fit the male pins in the plated holes. Check USB access and antenna keepouts.
  3. Fit the listed through-hole parts, then solder module pins from the underside. No female module sockets.
  4. Wire the finished isolated power and UART modules and the existing radios/sensors to the labeled carrier connectors.
  5. Check polarity, continuity and loaded rail voltages before connecting the full assembly.
  6. Commission one function at a time, then test all functions together including power loss and recovery.
Power and service: separate the controller supply paths from the peripheral 3.3 V rail. Do not parallel regulator outputs. Carrier diode drops require loaded-rail checks. USB service needs a verified VBUS-isolation procedure; merely unplugging PoE can leave back-power paths through signal pins.

Still requires the real assembly

  • Power and diode headroom
  • USB/back-power isolation in the real assembly
  • Mechanical fit and antenna spacing
  • RF coexistence and range
  • End-to-end ZHA / Thread / Z-Wave commissioning
  • Simultaneous workload reliability

One current layout reduces the choice and assembly burden. Board-specific firmware configuration and UART-transport hardening remain real work; no claim of zero software changes or guaranteed lower power is made.
